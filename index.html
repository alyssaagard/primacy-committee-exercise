The Primacy Premium

Committee Exercise

Two sided conditional play on the pathways question, 2026 to 2035

Alyssa Agard

This exercise puts two committees on opposite sides of the pathways question. Each period, both committees commit a stance sealed and simultaneously; the stances condition the forecast, they do not script the outcome. Indicators publish on their real schedules, so each committee reads the world late and partial, which is the condition the underlying research project is about. Play is not zero sum: each committee is scored against its own objectives, its commitment expenditure, and the calibration of the interval forecasts it states before each resolution.

Order of play

  1. Read the indicator board. Series publish on their real schedules, so parts of the record are provisional or not yet public.
  2. Set a stance in each of five categories. The committee estimate redraws live as stances change: that morphing fan is the conditioning, made visible.
  3. State an eighty percent interval for the war risk premium fixing. It is scored when the fixing publishes.
  4. Commit sealed. The counterpart commits without seeing your sheet; in two committee play the device is passed under seal.
  5. Resolution. Circulars issue, indicators tick to their new fixings, intervals score, and the pathway posterior updates. Five periods to 2035, then the debrief opens the sealed record.

Reference estimate and published path

The reference fan conditions all stances at reference values; it is the shared staff estimate, deliberately uninformed by either committee's sealed stance. Published values overlay in ink.

Interval scoring

Winkler interval score at eighty percent nominal coverage; lower is better. Width is paid always; misses are penalized at ten times the exceedance.

Pathway posterior

Classifier over conditioning trajectories, evaluated on committed stances and the episode record, sealed parameters at reference until the debrief.
